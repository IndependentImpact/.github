# Independent Impact

Independent Impact is a radically open platform for reporting, quantifying, and verifying human impact with transparency, community oversight, and verifiable technology.

Our work combines open methodologies, semantic standards, and practical tooling so communities can build trustworthy social and environmental impact systems.

## What we are building

- **Protocol and standards**: [Protocol](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/Protocol), [NovaImpactAccountingStandard](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/NovaImpactAccountingStandard)
- **Ontology and data models**: [Bhash](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/Bhash), [indicatorOntology](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/indicatorOntology), [methodologyOntology](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/methodologyOntology)
- **Tooling and integrations**: [ttl2d3](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/ttl2d3), [ttf2owl](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/ttf2owl), [Guardener](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/Guardener), [w3cli](https://github.com/IndependentImpact/w3cli)
